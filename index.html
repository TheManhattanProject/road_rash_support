Quick Support Note

Road R*sh is an offline arcade motorcycle combat game. Because the application does not connect to external servers or collect user accounts, all save data (unlocked districts, cash, bike upgrades, and celebrations) resides exclusively on your local device.

🎮 How to Play: Controls & Mechanics

Mastering arcade motorcycle combat in Road R*sh requires sharp reflexes and quick timing:

Action Touch Controls Mechanic Details
Throttle Automatic Your bike accelerates automatically up to top cruising speed.
Steering Left / Right Buttons or Road Drag Hold LEFT/RIGHT buttons or drag the roadway laterally to navigate lanes and avoid oncoming traffic.
Brake Tap / Hold BRAKE Instantly decelerates your motorcycle. Braking overrides Boost immediately when tapped.
Nitro Boost Tap BOOST Provides rapid burst acceleration to outpace pursuers and cross intersections before lights change.
Melee Attack Tap ATTACK Swings your equipped weapon (Baton, Chain, Folding Chair, or Flamethrower). Auto-targets the nearest rival. When flanked on both sides, consecutive hits alternate left and right.
Taunt / Celebrations Tap TAUNT Executes your equipped gesture (such as the SIUU victory celebration) while facing nearby rivals.
Screen Recording Tap REC Uses Apple ReplayKit to record gameplay and internal audio to local storage without mic or camera access.

Weapons Arsenal

  • Baton: Quick recovery, ideal for rapid consecutive strikes against tightly clustered opponents.
  • Chain: Extended melee reach to clip riders before they close in.
  • Folding Chair: High impact heavy hit that destabilizes rival riders quickly.
  • Flamethrower: Devastating burst damage that consumes fuel. Replenish fuel with on-track fuel pickups.

Hazards & Road Encounters

  • Cross-Traffic Intersections: Watch amber warnings and traffic signals. Vehicles cross laterally; plan your lane changes accordingly.
  • Police Pursuits: Patrols engage in announced pressure waves. Use nitro boosts to break away during recovery intervals.
  • Bank Getaway Incident: High-speed oncoming traffic scenario with advance warning and an escape lane.
  • Crashes & Recovery: Severe collisions trigger an arcade recovery cycle: slide, stand, lift bike, and remount. The clock keeps running!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is my game progress saved?

All game progress, earned cash, bike upgrades, unlocked districts, and custom celebrations are saved locally in your device's isolated application storage (using Apple's standard UserDefaults system). No cloud account is required.

Can I transfer my progress to another iPhone or iPad?

Yes. When you migrate your device using standard Apple Device Transfer (iOS Quick Start) or restore from an encrypted iCloud/Finder backup, your local Road R*sh save data transfers seamlessly with your device backup.

Are there any in-app purchases (IAP) or subscription fees?

No. Road R*sh contains zero microtransactions, loot boxes, or recurring subscriptions. All bikes, upgrades, weapons, and districts are unlocked solely through gameplay achievements and podium finishes.

Does Road R*sh display advertisements?

No. There are no banner ads, interstitial pop-ups, or rewarded video networks embedded in the game. It is a clean, distraction-free arcade racing experience.

How does the ReplayKit screen recording work?

When you tap the Record button, iOS prompts you to allow screen recording. ReplayKit records only the rendered game graphics and audio. The microphone and camera remain completely disabled. Recorded clips are saved temporarily in the app cache and can be exported to your Photos library or shared via the native iOS share sheet.

How do race highlight cards work?

At the end of a race, or following dramatic knockouts or celebrations, the game generates an export-ready 1080×1350 still highlight card. Tapping share opens the native iOS Share Sheet so you can save or send the image. Nothing is uploaded or posted automatically.

The game audio is silent. How do I fix this?

Check the hardware Ring/Silent switch on the side of your iPhone (or the Silent Mode control in Control Center / Action Button). In addition, ensure your in-game SFX and Engine synthesis volume sliders are turned up in the Settings / Pause menu.

What are the minimum system requirements?

Road R*sh is optimized for iOS 16.0 or later on iPhone and iPad, and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13.0 or later.

How do I reset my save game data?

Because all save data is stored in the application sandbox, uninstalling the app from your device and reinstalling it from the App Store will completely reset all local game data.

🛠️ Troubleshooting & Bug Reporting

If you encounter an unexpected issue, please review these quick checks:

  • Crash on Startup: Restart your iOS device and confirm you have at least 500 MB of free storage. Ensure your iOS version is up to date.
  • Steering Responsiveness: In the pause menu, you can toggle between on-screen button controls and roadway drag steering according to your preference.
  • Frame Drops or Heat: Close background resource-heavy apps. The game targets a smooth 60 FPS across supported devices.

Submit a Support Ticket or Bug Report

When reporting an issue, please email support@shortsqueeze.space with the following details so our team can resolve it quickly:

  1. Device Model: (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro, iPad Air 5th Gen)
  2. iOS Version: (e.g., iOS 17.5.1, iOS 18.0)
  3. Game District / Race: (e.g., District 1, District 2, Garage menu)
  4. Description: What happened, and what did you expect to happen?
  5. Steps to Reproduce: What actions led up to the issue?
  6. Attachments: Any screenshots or ReplayKit video clips (optional).
